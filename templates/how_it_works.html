How it Works

From form submission to risk score — a transparent pipeline.

01

Collect Inputs

You provide 19 attributes covering demographics, subscribed services, and billing details. All fields are required so the model receives a complete profile.

02

Prepare Features

Tenure is converted into categorical 12-month bins. Categorical columns are one-hot encoded. The resulting vector is aligned to the exact feature set the model was trained on.

03

Run the Model

A Decision Tree Classifier (scikit-learn) produces both a binary prediction (churn / stay) and a probability. The probability is shown as a confidence percentage.

04

Present the Result

You see a clear headline (“High Churn Risk” or “Customer Likely to Stay”) together with the confidence score. The form retains your inputs so you can tweak values and re-run instantly.

Why a Decision Tree?

Decision trees are easy to interpret and handle mixed categorical/numerical data well. For a demo-focused product they provide a good balance of accuracy and transparency. The model was trained offline; only the serialized artifact is shipped with the app.

No online learning or data collection happens at runtime — every prediction is independent and local.
