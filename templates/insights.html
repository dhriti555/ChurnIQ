Key Risk Signals

  • Short tenure — customers in their first year are more likely to leave.
  • Month-to-month contracts — flexibility often correlates with higher attrition.
  • Electronic check payments — associated with elevated churn in the training data.
  • No online security / tech support — lack of add-on services can signal weaker attachment.
  • High monthly charges — especially when combined with short tenure.

What the Model Sees

The decision tree evaluates combinations of the above factors (plus gender, partner status, dependents, streaming services, and more). It does not rely on a single rule — it learns interaction effects from the historical data.
